A real guru overcomes material attraction

1986年7月6日

The whole understanding of the cosmic consciousness can be put in one little sentence: that it cannot be attracted by the gravity of any stars, earth, moon or sun.

对宇宙意识的整个理解可以用一句话来表达: 它不能被任何恒星、地球、月亮或太阳的引力所吸引。

The other attractions one has from the Mother Earth are all for food, greed, lust – materialism at its worst.

来自地球母亲的其他吸引力都是食物，贪婪，欲望-最糟糕的唯物主义。

It comes from matter.

它来自物质。

All that comes from matter, once you start getting used to it you become a slave and not a guru.

所有这些都来自物质，一旦你开始习惯它，你就成了奴隶，而不是古鲁。

So the first attempt should be for anybody: to be a good guru is to get over material attraction.

因此，对于任何人来说，第一个尝试应该是: 成为一个好的大师就是克服物质吸引力。

In a subtler way we can see it happens like this: a lady or a gentleman buys an ashram in Sahaja Yoga.

在更微妙的方式中，我们可以看到它是这样发生的: 一位女士或一位绅士在霎哈嘉瑜伽中买了一个道场。

And a Sahaja yogi start living in that ashram.

一个霎哈嘉瑜伽士开始住在那道场。

The owner of that house starts worrying about the house and all material things in that house rather than the emancipation of the disciples or the Sahaja yogis who are there.

那房子的主人开始担心房子和房子里的所有物质，而不是那里的门徒或霎哈嘉瑜伽士的解放。

The whole attention is towards the material upkeep of the house and not towards the emancipation of the Sahaja yogis who are supposed to rise into the cosmic consciousness.

整个注意力都集中在房屋的物质维护上，而不是解放应该上升到宇宙意识中的The哈贾瑜伽士。

And such a person, if he starts believing that he is the guru or in any way even a Sahaja yogi, he is sadly mistaken.

这样的人，如果他开始相信自己是古鲁，或者以任何方式甚至是霎哈嘉瑜伽士，那他就大错特错了。

Where is your attention is the point.

你的注意力在哪里才是重点。

If you are the guru, where is your attention?

如果你是大师，你的注意力在哪里？

If your attention is on the correction and the nourishment of yourself and of others then you are first of all the Sahaja yogi.

如果你的注意力集中在纠正和滋养你自己和他人，那么你首先是霎哈嘉瑜珈士。

And once you rise above the gravitational force of materialism, then you could be called as the guru.

一旦你超越了唯物主义的引力，你就可以被称为大师。
