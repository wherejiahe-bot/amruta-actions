Fixing up priorities

优先级

1981-07-05

Only sometimes I find a conflict between family people.

仅在有些时候，我发现家庭成员之间有冲突。

Your priority should be sahaja yoga, sahaja yoga, sahaja yoga.

你的优先级应该是霎哈嘉瑜伽，霎哈嘉瑜伽，还是霎哈嘉瑜伽。

Say a husband may not be equally interested in sahaja yoga and the wife is pulled down.

比如说丈夫可能对霎哈嘉瑜伽并没有那么有兴趣，那么妻子就会被拉下来。

She should show that her interest is sahaja yoga nothing else and he will come.

她应该表明她的兴趣只在霎哈嘉瑜伽，没有其它，他就会进来。

She should not give way to him.

她不应该让步，她不应该向他让步。

And if he is so that he wants to get into sahaja yoga and she objects.

而如果是他想要进入霎哈嘉瑜伽，她却反对。

She has no business to do that under any divine law she has no business to do that.

她没有权利这么做，在任何神圣的律法之下，她都没有权利这么做。

If she tries that, husband need not listen to her at all he should go full ahead.

如果她试图那么做，丈夫根本不需要听她的，而只需全力以赴。

Because ultimately he is an important part of sahaja yoga.

因为最终他是霎哈嘉瑜伽的一个重要部分。

And he should not in any way make the importance less.

他不应该以任何方式降低其重要性。

So she should go ahead with what she wants.

所以她应该继续做她想做的事。

If he is a sahaja yogi he should not bother about anything.

如果他是一个霎哈嘉瑜伽士，他不应该为任何事烦恼。

His family will be blessed, everybody will be blessed, if he is there.

如果他在那里，他的家庭会得到祝福，所有人都会得到祝福。

So any excuses, that takes you away from sahaja yoga are not helpful to the family.

因此任何借口，任何把你带离霎哈嘉瑜伽的借口都对家庭没有帮助。

Because if the deities are angry your family will be harmed.

因为如果神祇生气了，你的家庭就会受到伤害。

If say, a woman is a sahaja-yogini and her family people object to it they will be harmed and she will be harmed as well.

如果说，一个女性是一位霎哈嘉瑜伽士，而她的家人反对霎哈嘉瑜伽，她的家人就会受到伤害，而她也会受到伤害。

But if she is in sahaja yoga all the family will be saved.

但是如果她在霎哈嘉瑜伽之中，那么整个家庭都会得到拯救。

So, you are doing a greater, you are doing a greater service to your family by becoming a sahaja yogi and a true sahaja yogi and a dedicated and devoted sahaja yogi.

所以你正做一件更伟大的事，你正在为你的家庭做更伟大的贡献，通过成为一名霎哈嘉瑜伽士，一名真正的霎哈嘉瑜伽士，一位虔诚奉献的霎哈嘉瑜伽士。

But no individualistic efforts, no individualistic attitude is helpful to Me or to sahaja yoga, is very detrimental under any name under any banner under any excuse.

但是任何个人主义的努力，任何个人主义的态度都不会对我或者是霎哈嘉瑜伽有所帮助，以任何名义、任何旗帜、任何借口都是非常有害的。

Try to find out ways and methods of becoming one with others.

尝试找出与他人融为一体的方式和方法。

If you want to find out faults with others there can be any, I mean I should not be here.

如果你想要找别人的错，可以是任何错误，我意思是我不应该在这里。

I can say that.

我可以这么说。

Then I should not be on this earth.

那么我也不应该在这个地球上。

I could have said that.

我本可以这么说。

But I see that spirit in you, and I see that beauty in you.

但是我看到了你内在的灵，我看到了你内在的美丽。

I don’t see other things.

我看不到其他东西。

If I had not seen that spirit I would not have worked like this.

如果我不曾看到灵，我就不会像这样工作。
